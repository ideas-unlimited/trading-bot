CAKE swing — which chart pays?

CAKE/USDT · 15m / 30m / 1h · 2025-11-28 → 2026-11-28 (12-month cap for sub-4h)

Long only, spot. Signal on the close, fill at the next open. 10 bps fee + 5 bps slippage per side, 3% risk per trade, $1,000 start. Drawdown kill switch off so every chart gets the whole window.

One chart pays, and the payment is three trades. The rule below turns +34% on 30m against −36% for holding CAKE, but −9.9% on 15m and −3.5% on 1h, and 99% of the 30m profit comes from three September trades. That is an uptrend the rule happened to be long for, not a repeated edge.

The rule

  • Buy when RSI(14) crosses back above 30 on a close above the 50-day EMA (the gate).
  • Stop 3 ATR(14) below the entry, then trail 5 ATR under the highest high since entry.
  • Out anyway after 72 hours. No target, no shorting.

Selection score is the worse of two IS halves, so a cell that only worked in one half cannot win. Three families (breakout, pullback, dip) × two lookbacks × two stops × four exits, plus donchian, momentum_swing and rsi_meanrev with the same windows in hours. No cell on any chart is positive in both halves.

Note what that means for the rule above: it is not the sweep’s best cell. It was picked on the full-window result, which includes the OOS half — the exact mistake this split exists to catch. Treat it as a hypothesis that earned a forward test, not as a finding.

Honest reading

  • CAKE fell 36% over the window. Any long-only swing rule starts 36 points behind and can only win by sitting out, which is what the 50-day gate does.
  • 30m is the only chart where the engine of the move is visible: 47 trades, 40% win rate, median trade negative, fat right tail from a handful of continuation runs.
  • 15m loses money at 107 trades even with the same rule — costs and chop, not signal.
  • The same rule on 4h or 1d over two years takes 0–2 trades: on a coarser chart the dip never coincides with the gate, so the 30m result depends on intrabar noise.
  • Therefore: not a shippable sleeve. It is a candidate to forward-test on paper, sized at the 3% the lab used, and it must survive a second CAKE rally it did not see.
